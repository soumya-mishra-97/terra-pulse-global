# Net migration (SM.POP.NETM)

Net migration is the number of immigrants minus the number of emigrants, including both citizens and non-citizens. A positive value means more people arrived than left during the period; a negative value means net out-migration.

Migration is among the hardest demographic components to measure. Many countries do not register emigration, and estimates are often derived indirectly as the residual between population change and natural increase. Values for small countries and for countries affected by conflict or displacement carry particularly large uncertainty and can be revised substantially between releases.

Net migration does not show gross flows: a country can have large inflows and outflows that roughly cancel out. Refugee movements, temporary labour migration and undocumented migration may be captured inconsistently across countries.
