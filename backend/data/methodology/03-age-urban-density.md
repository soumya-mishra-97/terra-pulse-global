# Age structure

WDI publishes the share of the total population aged 0-14 (SP.POP.0014.TO.ZS), 15-64 (SP.POP.1564.TO.ZS) and 65 and above (SP.POP.65UP.TO.ZS). TerraPulse derives the age dependency ratio as the number of people aged 0-14 and 65+ per 100 people aged 15-64. A falling share of children combined with a rising share of people aged 65+ is a typical sign of population ageing.

# Urban and rural population

Urban population (SP.URB.TOTL.IN.ZS) refers to people living in urban areas as defined by national statistical offices. Rural population (SP.RUR.TOTL.ZS) is the remainder. Because each country defines "urban" differently, urbanisation levels are not strictly comparable across countries; trends within one country are more reliable than cross-country rankings.

# Population density and land area

Population density (EN.POP.DNST) is midyear population divided by land area in square kilometres. Land area (AG.LND.TOTL.K2) excludes inland water bodies. National density averages hide very large differences between regions within a country. TerraPulse does not hold sub-national data, so it cannot describe geographic distribution within a country beyond the national average.
